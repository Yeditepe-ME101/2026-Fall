* Assignment Instructions
** 1. Open an account in Overleaf.
1. Go to [[https://www.overleaf.com]].
2. Create a new account linked to your student email.
3. Login to your new  Overleaf account.

** 2. Create a copy of the report template.
1. Click on the [[https://www.overleaf.com/read/vnvpczskjydh#efda08][report template link]].
2. Make a copy of this template and name it, e.g. "me101-classwork-1D"
3. Find the lines that define the professor and author(s), and make required changes reflecting your group members.
4. Polish the story that you submitted to [[https://yulearn.yeditepe.edu.tr/course/view.php?id=44037#][Sectoral Awareness]] assignment, and move it to your new report.
5. Investigate the references.bib file, and create new entries for your report references.
6. Make sure you have at least one equation, one table and one figure, and to cite them in your manuscript.
7. Don't forget to include citations to your  references in the manuscript. 

** 3. Compile the document (LaTeX only)
1. Click on the =Compile= button in Overleaf.
2. This will compile your =main.tex= file, and displayed the resulting PDF in the viewer.
3. Error are also shown in a separate tab above the viewer window.

** 4. Download/create a PDF and submit it.
1. Confirm that your PDF reflects the necessary nae changes.
2. Download the PDF into an appropriate directory in your filesystem.
3. Upload the PDF file to the [[https://yulearn.yeditepe.edu.tr/mod/assign/view.php?id=295881][Homework Assignment]] in Yulearn.
